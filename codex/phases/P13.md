batch. This is a new local expansion phase, not a production release.

schedule failures. Provide unread counts, idempotent read acknowledgement and
safe navigation that respects current holdout/erasure access. No email, Slack or
other external messaging is authorized.

meaningful real-service/security/browser tests, worker-restart/duplicate-tick
proof, screenshots, BUILD_LOG and feature matrix updates, plus a local PR draft.

historical evidence. Production and optional dependency blockers remain explicit.
